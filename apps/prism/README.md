# prism

**[Download for Android (APK)](https://github.com/isene/nomad/releases/download/apk/prism.apk)** · [how to install](https://isene.org/nomad/#get)

The colour picker of the laptop's [prism](https://github.com/isene/prism), on the phone.

- Two colours, FG and BG. Tap one to change it.
- R, G, B and H, S, V sliders on the same colour. − and + step by 1, by 10 on a long press.
- V past 100 goes on toward white, as on the laptop.
- Six hex digits set a colour.
- Sample text in FG on BG, with the contrast of the pair and its WCAG level.
- Copy the colour as hex, rgb, hsv or all three; tap the format to change it.
- The two colours are kept for the next start.
- No permissions, no network, no Rust core.

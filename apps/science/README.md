# science

The science apps of the [Fe₂O₃ suite](https://isene.org/fe2o3/) in one app on the phone, a tab each.

## Elements

- The periodic table, 118 elements and the eight that are only predicted.
- Each family has its own colour.
- Tap an element for its facts and a short text about it.
- Article fetches the whole Wikipedia article and keeps it on the phone.
- Isotopes shows the element on the chart of the nuclides.

## Isotopes

- The chart of the nuclides: 3,386 isotopes from the IAEA table.
- Neutrons go across and protons go up.
- The colour says how an isotope decays; white is stable.
- Tap a square for the half-life, the decay modes and the share in nature.
- Under it is a list of every isotope of the same element.

## Particles

- The Standard Model: 17 particles, and the proton and the neutron built from them.
- Each kind has its own colour.
- Tap one for its mass, charge, spin, the forces it feels and its Wikipedia article.

## Stars

- The Hertzsprung-Russell diagram of 461 named stars, each in its own colour.
- Hot stars are to the left and bright stars at the top.
- Tap a star for its facts, a short text and its Wikipedia article.

## Planets

- 6,309 planets around other stars, from the NASA table, and our own eight for scale.
- Far from the star is to the right, and big is at the top.
- The colour says how a planet was found.
- Tap one for its orbit, size, temperature and habitable zone.
- Under it is a list of the planets around the same star.

## Universe

- 37 steps from the quantum foam to the whole universe we can see, a picture for each.
- Smaller and Larger step along the ladder; a tap on the ruler jumps.
- It opens on a person, in the middle.

Two fingers zoom the table, the chart and the two diagrams, and one finger moves them.

The data is in the app, so every tab works with no network.
The app reaches the network only when Article is tapped.

The same data as the laptop's [elements](https://github.com/isene/elements), [isotopes](https://github.com/isene/isotopes), [particles](https://github.com/isene/particles), [stars](https://github.com/isene/stars), [exoplanets](https://github.com/isene/exoplanets) and [universe](https://github.com/isene/universe).
